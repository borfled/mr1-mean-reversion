# MR-1 Mean-Reversion Research

Ongoing quantitative trading research by David Le, a finance student at the University of North Florida.

## Research approach

The project explores a market-neutral U.S. equity strategy that buys short-term losers and shorts short-term winners across approximately 500 stocks, scaling exposure with VIX.

The research uses Python, QuantConnect, Claude Code, and Codex. Its experiment workflow examines trading costs, borrow costs, and universe assumptions one variant at a time. Deflated Sharpe and probability-of-overfitting (PBO) tests are part of the evaluation approach described in my resume.

This repository currently contains the project overview. Strategy source code, reproducible experiment instructions, and reviewed results still need to be added. No performance results are published here.
